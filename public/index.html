Questions

Which cities do you cover?

Ahmedabad, Gandhinagar and Mumbai.

What do you collect?

Paper, cardboard, plastic, metal and e-waste — from newspapers and cartons to ACs, fridges and laptops.

How do I book?

Add your items above, choose a day and time, then add your address and phone number. You'll need a free account to confirm.

Do you pick up from businesses?

Yes. Shops, offices, restaurants and warehouses can get scheduled pickups and monthly reports. Get a waste plan.

What is Report Waste for?

Only for waste you spot on a road, footpath or open plot. For your own scrap, book a pickup.
